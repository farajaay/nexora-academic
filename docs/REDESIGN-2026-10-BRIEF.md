## Specificity review

Would I produce this same plan for any other app? The early academic-only
interpretation would have misrepresented the current product. The final design
retains the seven digital services, the industrial consulting practice and the
separate academic pricing and request flows. The service index links design and
engineering rather than inventing a generic agency feature grid.

The owner's supplied DESIGN.md supersedes earlier visual directions in
REDESIGN-V2-BRIEF.md. The existing dark default, visitor mode preference,
Arabic/English routes, academic pricing and Supabase behavior are retained.
Implementation is on a review branch; the existing merge approval requirement
still applies.

## Color legend

- `paper`: reading surface and inverse text on actions.
- `ink`: primary content, including errors identified explicitly in words.
- `ink-2`: supporting text and explanations.
- `line`: boundaries that group content and define form controls.
- `accent`: actionable controls and their selected states only.
- `signal`: the confirmed database-save acknowledgement only; never a promise
  of submission when a messaging app merely opened.

No per-project hues, status dots or decorative accent fields. The success
acknowledgement is the only celebration, with one short animation. Loading
indicators describe work in progress; all decisions wait without animation.
Reduced motion retains text and borders.

## Shape and typography

System sans for Latin; the device-safe Arabic stack for Arabic. No font download.
Page type uses a 1.333 ratio. Inputs use 4px corners; document panels are square;
only compact filter tags use a pill radius. Spacing follows a 4px scale.

## Honest presentation

Portfolio entries remain explicitly labelled concepts. No clients, outcomes,
statistics, testimonials or availability claims are invented. The services and
pricing pages explain their true limits. WhatsApp prepares a message and does
not itself submit or save the brief. Email remains disabled until configured.

## Deliberate subtraction

Removed the decorative laptop/phone illustration and its floating chip. Also
removed gradient project covers, highlighted headline words, and decorative
heading icons. The service list and proposed deliverables now carry the detail.
