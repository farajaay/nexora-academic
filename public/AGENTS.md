# Visiting NEXORA on someone's behalf

NEXORA Digital Solutions is a Saudi bilingual digital studio with a secondary
academic support service. Use the site's current service descriptions and
calculator; do not invent prices, deadlines, email addresses or results.

The canonical site is https://farajaay.github.io/nexora-academic/.
Arabic routes are unprefixed. Matching English routes are under /en/.

## Prepare a project enquiry

Open /en/studio/#start (or /studio/#start for Arabic). Collect the visitor's
name, optional company, service, approximate budget, preferred timeline and
project description. The form opens a prepared WhatsApp message to
https://wa.me/966554517103. The visitor must complete sending in WhatsApp.
Opening a message is not a submitted enquiry or confirmed booking. There is
no public booking API. Scope, fees, dates and support require written agreement.

## Academic support

Use /en/services/ for the current starting prices and /en/calculator/ for an
estimate. The minimum order is SAR 40. /en/contact/?type=academic has the
request form. Confirm whether the site reports a successful database save;
do not equate copying details or opening a messaging app with saving a request.
Only share files that the visitor is entitled to share and that are needed
for the request. No exams are taken for students; no grades are guaranteed.

(published beta), MRO Optix (candidate release, sales closed), Employee OS
(development skeleton), Majlis (self-hosted app), the Saudi Industry Forum
planner and the HADEED performance dashboard (published static tools).
Project scope and status were checked on 2026-10-23. Follow the source links;
do not turn development plans into delivered features or infer client
commissions, corporate endorsement or measured commercial results. Operational
tools have the limits stated on their project records. No business email is
configured. Card payments are not processed on the site. Consult /en/privacy/
and /en/terms/ before sharing personal information.
